---
title: "Reporting An Issue"
description: "Report a bug or unexpected behavior in RocketSim. Use the built-in issue reporter in Settings to send diagnostic information directly to the development team."
---

I’m eager to help you out, and it's my priority to solve user-reported issues. Go into **Settings → RocketSim Pro → Report a bug**, or choose **Report an issue…** from RocketSim's menu bar menu, and email any details you can share for your issue.
