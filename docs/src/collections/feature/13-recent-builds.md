Quick actions for recent builds keep repeatable tests close to the active Simulator or connected physical iOS device. Actions follow the selected app's bundle identifier and target capabilities.

- **Launch, relaunch, terminate, or uninstall** apps on physical devices
- **Relaunch with locale** to test supported languages without changing device settings
- Open saved **deep links** and simulate **locations** on Xcode 27 devices
- On Simulator, travel the world and **relaunch with any time zone**
- **Grant, revoke, or reset permissions** like photo and location access, allowing you to quicker test related implementations
- **Open common directories** like your app's documents folder.
