Bring repeatable app testing to real hardware without leaving your Mac.

- Open saved **deep links and Universal Links** on a connected iPhone or iPad
- Simulate **coordinates and routes** through Xcode 27
- Test automatic **system time-zone changes** from simulated locations
- Launch, relaunch, terminate, localize, or uninstall a recent device build
